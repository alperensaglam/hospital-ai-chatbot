# City General Hospital — Frequently Asked Questions

## What are the hospital's visiting hours?
Visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. Each patient may have up to two visitors at a time. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult at all times. During flu season or pandemic conditions, visiting may be restricted — please check the hospital website or call the information desk before your visit.

## Where is the hospital located?
City General Hospital is located at 125 Healthcare Boulevard, Suite A, Downtown District. The main entrance is on Healthcare Boulevard, and the emergency entrance is on the south side of the building via Urgent Care Lane. Free parking is available in Lot B for outpatient visitors.

## How do I schedule an appointment?
You can schedule an appointment through our online patient portal, by calling our central appointment line at (555) 100-2000, or by visiting the reception desk in person. Online appointments can be booked up to 30 days in advance. Same-day appointments are available for urgent but non-emergency cases on a first-come, first-served basis.

## What should I bring to my first appointment?
Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications, and any relevant medical records or referral letters. Arriving 15 minutes early is recommended to complete registration paperwork.

## Does the hospital accept my insurance?
City General Hospital accepts most major insurance plans, including BlueCross BlueShield, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, Cigna, and Medicare. For a full list of accepted plans, please contact our billing department at (555) 100-2005 or check the insurance section on our website.

## What do I do in a medical emergency?
For life-threatening emergencies, call 911 immediately. Our Emergency Department is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Walk-in patients are triaged based on severity. Average wait times for non-critical cases range from 30 to 90 minutes depending on department load.

## Can I get my medical records?
Yes. You can request your medical records through the patient portal or by submitting a written request to the Health Information Management (HIM) office. Processing typically takes 5-7 business days. There may be a small fee for printed copies.

## Is there a pharmacy on site?
Yes. The City General Hospital Pharmacy is located on the ground floor near the main lobby. It is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. The pharmacy fills both inpatient and outpatient prescriptions.

## Does the hospital offer language interpretation services?
Yes. We provide professional medical interpreters in over 20 languages, available in person or via video call. Please inform the front desk or your care team if you require interpretation services. American Sign Language (ASL) interpreters are also available upon 24-hour advance request.

## What is the hospital's patient feedback process?
We welcome all feedback. You can submit comments through the patient portal, speak with a Patient Relations representative on the second floor, or call (555) 100-2010. All complaints are reviewed within 48 hours and you will receive a written response within 10 business days.
