# City General Hospital — Insurance and Coverage Policy

## Accepted Insurance Plans
City General Hospital is an in-network provider for the following insurance plans:
- BlueCross BlueShield (all regional plans)
- Aetna (PPO and HMO)
- UnitedHealthcare (PPO, HMO, and Medicare Advantage)
- Cigna (PPO and Open Access)
- Medicare (Parts A and B)
- Medicaid (state-administered plans)
- Humana (PPO and HMO)
- Kaiser Permanente (referral-based only)
- TRICARE (active military and veterans)

For plans not listed above, please contact our insurance verification team at (555) 100-2005 to check coverage before your appointment.

## Insurance Verification Process
- We recommend verifying your insurance coverage at least 48 hours before your appointment.
- Bring your current insurance card to every visit.
- If your insurance plan has changed since your last visit, inform the registration desk immediately.
- For specialist visits, confirm with your insurer whether a referral or prior authorization is required.

## Copays and Deductibles
- Copays are collected at the time of service (check-in).
- If you have not met your annual deductible, you may be responsible for a larger portion of the charges.
- The hospital will bill your insurance first. Any remaining patient responsibility will be billed to you within 30 days.

## Prior Authorization
Certain services require prior authorization from your insurance company before they can be performed. These commonly include:
- Advanced imaging (MRI, CT scan with contrast)
- Elective surgical procedures
- Specialty referrals (depending on your plan type)
- Certain prescription medications

Our authorization team will work with your insurance company to obtain approval. This process may take 3-5 business days. Please plan accordingly.

## Out-of-Network Services
If City General Hospital is out-of-network for your insurance plan:
- You may still receive services, but your out-of-pocket costs will likely be higher.
- The No Surprises Act protects patients from surprise bills for emergency services and certain non-emergency services at in-network facilities performed by out-of-network providers.
- You will receive a Good Faith Estimate of costs before non-emergency out-of-network services are rendered.

## Financial Assistance for Underinsured Patients
Patients with high deductibles or limited coverage may qualify for:
- Sliding-scale fee reductions based on household income.
- Charity care programs for qualifying individuals and families.
- Interest-free payment plans for balances over $500.

Contact the Financial Counseling office at (555) 100-2006 for assistance.
