# City General Hospital — Visitor and General Policies

## Visiting Hours
- General visiting hours: 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM daily.
- ICU / Critical Care visiting: 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM (limited to immediate family).
- Pediatric ward: One parent or guardian may stay overnight. Second visitor allowed during general visiting hours.
- Maternity ward: The birth partner may stay 24 hours. Other visitors during general visiting hours only.

## Visitor Limits
- Maximum two visitors per patient at a time in general wards.
- Maximum one visitor at a time in ICU.
- Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult and are not permitted in ICU.
- During infectious disease outbreaks, visiting may be further restricted.

## Smoking Policy
City General Hospital is a smoke-free campus. Smoking, vaping, and the use of tobacco products are prohibited in all indoor and outdoor areas of the hospital grounds. Designated smoking cessation resources are available through the Wellness Office.

## Parking
- Free parking is available in Lot B (outpatient visitors) and Lot C (employees).
- Valet parking is available at the main entrance, Monday through Friday, $10 per vehicle.
- Handicapped parking spaces are located near all building entrances.
- Emergency Department parking is available in the designated area on the south side.

## Food Services
- The hospital cafeteria is located on the first floor and is open from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM daily.
- A coffee shop is located in the main lobby, open 6:30 AM to 5:00 PM weekdays.
- Vending machines are available on every floor, 24/7.
- Patients on restricted diets will receive meals planned by the hospital's dietitians.

## Wi-Fi
Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the hospital. Connect to "CityGeneral-Guest" network. No password is required. Do not use the hospital Wi-Fi for transmitting sensitive personal information.

## Lost and Found
Report lost items to the Security Office on the ground floor or call (555) 100-2020. Items are held for 30 days.
