# City General Hospital — Department Descriptions

## Cardiology Department
The Cardiology Department specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of heart and cardiovascular conditions. Services include echocardiography, stress testing, cardiac catheterization, electrophysiology studies, and heart failure management. The department is equipped with a state-of-the-art cardiac catheterization lab and a dedicated cardiac intensive care unit (CICU). Referrals from primary care physicians are recommended but not required for outpatient consultations. The department operates Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, with emergency cardiac care available 24/7 through the Emergency Department.

## Orthopedics Department
The Orthopedics Department provides comprehensive musculoskeletal care including joint replacement surgery, sports medicine, fracture management, spine surgery, and hand surgery. The department features advanced imaging facilities, a dedicated physical therapy rehabilitation center, and a minimally invasive surgery suite. Walk-in clinics are available for acute injuries on weekday mornings. Elective consultations require an appointment. The department is located on the third floor, Wing C.

## Neurology Department
The Neurology Department evaluates and treats disorders of the brain, spinal cord, and nervous system. Common conditions treated include stroke, epilepsy, multiple sclerosis, Parkinson's disease, migraines, and neuropathies. The department offers EEG monitoring, nerve conduction studies, and a comprehensive stroke center with rapid-response protocols. Telehealth consultations are available for follow-up visits. Operating hours are Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM.

## Pediatrics Department
The Pediatrics Department provides medical care for infants, children, and adolescents up to age 18. Services include well-child checkups, immunizations, developmental screenings, and management of acute and chronic childhood illnesses. The department has a child-friendly waiting area and playroom. A pediatric emergency section is co-located within the main Emergency Department with specialized pediatric nurses available around the clock. Appointments can be booked online or via phone.

## General Surgery Department
The General Surgery Department performs a wide range of surgical procedures including appendectomies, hernia repairs, gallbladder removal, and various abdominal surgeries. The department supports both open and laparoscopic minimally invasive techniques. Pre-surgical consultations are mandatory. Patients receive detailed pre-operative instructions and are assigned a surgical coordinator for scheduling. The operating suite is located on the fourth floor.

## Emergency Department
The Emergency Department (ED) is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. It handles all acute medical conditions, trauma cases, and psychiatric emergencies. The ED includes a rapid triage area, trauma bays, a fast-track section for minor injuries, and a dedicated pediatric emergency zone. Average wait time for non-critical cases is approximately 45 minutes, though this varies based on patient volume. Patients are seen in order of medical severity, not arrival time.

## Radiology Department
The Radiology Department offers a full suite of diagnostic imaging services including X-ray, ultrasound, CT scan, MRI, mammography, and fluoroscopy. Contrast-enhanced studies require prior scheduling and may require fasting. Results are typically available to the ordering physician within 24 hours. Walk-in X-ray services are available for emergency and urgent cases. The department is on the ground floor, Wing A.

## Internal Medicine Department
The Internal Medicine Department provides primary and specialized care for adult patients. Services include annual wellness exams, chronic disease management (diabetes, hypertension, COPD), preventive screenings, and referral coordination. The department also staffs the hospital's inpatient medical units. Appointments can be scheduled online, and same-day sick visits are available on a limited basis.
