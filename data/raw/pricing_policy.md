## Payment Options
- **Insurance:** We accept most major insurance plans. Co-pays are due at the time of service.
- **Self-Pay Discount:** Uninsured patients paying in full at the time of service receive a 20% discount on standard charges.
- **Payment Plans:** Interest-free payment plans are available for bills exceeding $500. Arrangements must be made with the billing department within 30 days of receiving a statement.
- **Financial Assistance:** Patients who meet income eligibility criteria may qualify for reduced-cost or charity care. Applications are available at the billing office or online.

## Billing Inquiries
Contact the billing department at (555) 100-2005 or email billing@citygeneralhospital.example.com. Billing representatives are available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

## Important Notes
- All prices listed are estimates and may vary based on individual clinical needs.
- The hospital provides a Good Faith Estimate for scheduled services upon request, as required by the No Surprises Act.
- Emergency services are provided regardless of ability to pay.
