# City General Hospital — Telehealth Services Guide

## What is Telehealth?
Telehealth allows you to have a medical consultation with your doctor via secure video call from the comfort of your home. It is suitable for follow-up visits, medication reviews, and many non-emergency consultations.

## Available Departments for Telehealth
- Internal Medicine
- Neurology (follow-up visits)
- Pediatrics (non-urgent consultations)
- Psychiatry and Behavioral Health

## How to Schedule a Telehealth Appointment
1. Log in to the patient portal at portal.citygeneralhospital.example.com.
2. Select "Schedule Appointment" and choose "Telehealth Visit."
3. Choose your department and preferred physician.
4. Select an available date and time.
5. You will receive a confirmation email with a secure video link.

## Technical Requirements
- A device with a camera and microphone (smartphone, tablet, or computer).
- A stable internet connection (minimum 5 Mbps recommended).
- A quiet, private location for your consultation.
- The latest version of Chrome, Safari, or Firefox browser.

## Telehealth Visit Fees
Telehealth consultations are priced at $100 - $175 for self-pay patients. Most insurance plans cover telehealth visits at the same rate as in-person visits. Verify with your insurer before your appointment.

## Limitations
Telehealth is not appropriate for:
- Emergency medical conditions
- Conditions requiring physical examination or hands-on procedures
- New patient visits to certain specialty departments
- Situations where diagnostic imaging or lab work is needed immediately
