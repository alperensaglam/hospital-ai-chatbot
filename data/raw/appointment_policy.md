# City General Hospital — Appointment Policy

## Scheduling Appointments
Appointments can be scheduled through the following channels:
- **Online Patient Portal:** Available 24/7 for registered patients. Appointments can be booked up to 30 days in advance.
- **Phone:** Call the central scheduling line at (555) 100-2000, available Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
- **In Person:** Visit the registration desk at the main lobby during business hours.

## Types of Appointments
- **New Patient Visit:** First-time visits with a physician typically last 45-60 minutes. Additional paperwork and registration time of 15-20 minutes should be expected.
- **Follow-Up Visit:** Subsequent visits are typically 15-30 minutes depending on the complexity of the case.
- **Same-Day Urgent Visit:** Available on a limited, first-come-first-served basis for conditions that need prompt attention but are not emergencies. Same-day slots open at 7:00 AM each morning.
- **Telehealth Visit:** Virtual consultations are available for select departments (Neurology, Internal Medicine, Pediatrics). Patients must have a stable internet connection and a device with camera and microphone.

## Referrals
Some specialist departments require a referral from a primary care physician or another specialist. Departments that typically require referral include:
- Cardiology (recommended but not mandatory)
- Neurology (required for new patients)
- Orthopedics — Sports Medicine (recommended)

The Emergency Department, Pediatrics, and Internal Medicine do not require referrals.

## Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy
- Appointments must be cancelled or rescheduled at least 24 hours in advance.
- Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment may incur a no-show fee of $50.
- Three consecutive no-shows within a 12-month period may result in discharge from the physician's patient panel.
- To cancel or reschedule, call (555) 100-2000 or use the patient portal.

## Late Arrival Policy
- Patients who arrive more than 15 minutes late for their appointment may need to reschedule.
- The decision to accommodate late arrivals is at the discretion of the physician and clinic schedule.

## Insurance Verification
- Insurance verification is recommended at least 48 hours before your appointment.
- If your insurance plan changes, notify the scheduling team before your visit to avoid billing issues.
- Co-pays and deductibles are collected at the time of the visit.
