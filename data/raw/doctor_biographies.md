# City General Hospital — Doctor Biographies

## Dr. Sarah Mitchell — Cardiology
Dr. Sarah Mitchell is a board-certified cardiologist with over 15 years of experience in interventional cardiology and heart failure management. She completed her residency at Johns Hopkins Hospital and her cardiology fellowship at the Cleveland Clinic. Dr. Mitchell specializes in cardiac catheterization, coronary stenting, and echocardiography. She sees patients Monday, Wednesday, and Friday mornings. She is accepting new patients with or without referral.

## Dr. James Park — Cardiology
Dr. James Park is an electrophysiologist specializing in heart rhythm disorders, pacemaker implantation, and atrial fibrillation management. He trained at Stanford University Medical Center and has published over 30 peer-reviewed articles. Dr. Park sees patients on Tuesdays and Thursdays. He accepts referrals from primary care physicians and cardiologists.

## Dr. Emily Chen — Orthopedics
Dr. Emily Chen is an orthopedic surgeon specializing in hip and knee joint replacement. She completed her fellowship in adult reconstruction at the Hospital for Special Surgery in New York. Dr. Chen uses minimally invasive and robotic-assisted surgical techniques. She holds clinic hours Monday through Thursday and performs surgeries on Fridays. She is accepting new patients.

## Dr. Michael Okonkwo — Orthopedics / Sports Medicine
Dr. Michael Okonkwo is a sports medicine specialist focusing on ACL reconstruction, rotator cuff repair, and arthroscopic surgery. He served as a team physician for a professional soccer club before joining City General Hospital. He sees patients Monday through Wednesday and is available for acute injury walk-ins on Thursday mornings.

## Dr. Aisha Rahman — Neurology
Dr. Aisha Rahman is a neurologist specializing in stroke prevention and treatment, epilepsy, and neurodegenerative diseases. She leads the hospital's Comprehensive Stroke Center and directs the EEG laboratory. Dr. Rahman completed her training at Massachusetts General Hospital. She sees patients Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays. Telehealth follow-ups are available.

## Dr. Robert Kim — Pediatrics
Dr. Robert Kim is a board-certified pediatrician with 12 years of experience in general pediatrics, childhood immunizations, and developmental assessments. He completed his residency at Children's National Medical Center. Dr. Kim sees patients Monday through Friday and is known for his patient, family-centered approach. Walk-in sick visits are available daily until 11:00 AM.

## Dr. Lisa Fernandez — General Surgery
Dr. Lisa Fernandez is a general surgeon with expertise in laparoscopic cholecystectomy, hernia repair, and appendectomy. She trained at the University of California San Francisco and has performed over 3,000 minimally invasive procedures. She holds pre-surgical consultations on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Operating days are Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

## Dr. David Nguyen — Internal Medicine
Dr. David Nguyen is an internist specializing in chronic disease management, preventive medicine, and geriatric care. He completed his training at the Mayo Clinic. Dr. Nguyen manages conditions including diabetes, hypertension, and COPD. He accepts walk-in appointments on Monday mornings and sees scheduled patients Tuesday through Friday.

## Dr. Priya Sharma — Radiology
Dr. Priya Sharma is a diagnostic radiologist with subspecialty training in musculoskeletal and body imaging. She reads and interprets all major imaging modalities including MRI, CT, and ultrasound. Dr. Sharma completed her fellowship at the University of Pennsylvania. She provides same-day consultation for complex imaging cases.

## Dr. Thomas Weber — Emergency Medicine
Dr. Thomas Weber is a board-certified emergency medicine physician and the current Director of the Emergency Department. He has over 20 years of experience in acute care, trauma resuscitation, and emergency ultrasound. Dr. Weber leads the department's quality improvement and rapid triage programs.
