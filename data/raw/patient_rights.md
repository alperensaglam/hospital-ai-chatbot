# City General Hospital — Patient Rights and Responsibilities

## Your Rights as a Patient

### Right to Quality Care
You have the right to receive considerate, respectful, and quality medical care regardless of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, age, disability, or ability to pay.

### Right to Information
You have the right to receive complete and current information about your diagnosis, treatment, and prognosis in terms you can understand. Interpretation services are provided at no charge.

### Right to Privacy and Confidentiality
Your medical records and personal health information are confidential and protected under HIPAA. Information will only be disclosed with your written authorization, except as required by law.

### Right to Informed Consent
Before any procedure, you have the right to receive a full explanation of the proposed treatment, its risks, benefits, alternatives, and the consequences of refusing treatment.

### Right to Participate in Care Decisions
You have the right to participate in all decisions regarding your healthcare, including the right to request a second opinion.

### Right to Advance Directives
You have the right to create advance directives specifying your wishes regarding medical treatment if you become unable to communicate.

### Right to Complain
You have the right to voice complaints about your care without fear of retaliation via the Patient Relations office.

## Your Responsibilities as a Patient

### Provide Accurate Information
Provide accurate and complete information about your health status, medical history, medications, and allergies.

### Follow Treatment Plans
Follow the treatment plan recommended by your healthcare team, including medications and follow-up appointments.

### Respect Others
Treat hospital staff, other patients, and visitors with courtesy and respect.

### Financial Obligations
Meet your financial obligations including copays, deductibles, and balances not covered by insurance.
