# City General Hospital — Surgery Preparation Instructions

## Before Your Surgery

### Pre-Operative Consultation
All elective surgery patients must attend a pre-operative consultation at least 7 days before the scheduled surgery date. During this visit, your surgeon will review the procedure, discuss risks and benefits, and answer your questions. You will also meet with an anesthesiologist if your surgery requires general anesthesia.

### Required Pre-Operative Tests
Depending on your age and medical history, you may need the following tests before surgery:
- Blood work (complete blood count, metabolic panel, coagulation studies)
- Chest X-ray (for patients over 50 or with respiratory conditions)
- ECG/EKG (for patients over 40 or with cardiac history)
- Urinalysis
- COVID-19 or respiratory panel test (if required by current hospital policy)

All pre-operative tests must be completed at least 3 business days before your surgery to allow time for result review.

### Medications
- Inform your surgical team of ALL medications you take, including over-the-counter drugs, vitamins, and herbal supplements.
- Blood-thinning medications (warfarin, aspirin, clopidogrel) must typically be stopped 5-7 days before surgery. Your surgeon will provide specific instructions.
- Diabetes medications may require dose adjustment on the day of surgery. Consult your surgeon or anesthesiologist.
- Take only the medications your surgical team has approved on the morning of surgery, with a small sip of water.

### Fasting Instructions
- Do NOT eat solid food for at least 8 hours before your surgery.
- Clear liquids (water, clear apple juice, black coffee without cream) may be consumed up to 2 hours before your scheduled arrival time.
- Do not chew gum or consume candy on the day of surgery.

### What to Bring
- Valid photo ID and insurance card.
- A list of your current medications and dosages.
- Advance directive or living will, if you have one.
- Comfortable, loose-fitting clothing for after the procedure.
- A responsible adult who can drive you home (required for all outpatient procedures involving sedation or general anesthesia).

### What NOT to Bring
- Jewelry, watches, or piercings (remove all before arrival).
- Large amounts of cash or valuables.
- Contact lenses (bring glasses instead).
- Nail polish or artificial nails (interferes with monitoring devices).

## Day of Surgery
1. Arrive at the Surgical Admissions area at your scheduled check-in time (usually 2 hours before the procedure).
2. You will be checked in, given a hospital gown, and have an IV line placed.
3. Your surgical team will verify your identity, the procedure, and the surgical site (this is a standard safety protocol).
4. After the procedure, you will be taken to the Post-Anesthesia Care Unit (PACU) for recovery monitoring.
5. Discharge instructions will be provided before you leave, including medication prescriptions, wound care guidance, and follow-up appointment scheduling.

## After Surgery
- Follow all discharge instructions carefully.
- Do not drive or operate heavy machinery for 24 hours after receiving anesthesia.
- Attend all scheduled follow-up appointments.
- Contact your surgeon's office immediately if you experience fever above 101°F, excessive bleeding, increasing pain not controlled by prescribed medication, or signs of infection at the surgical site.
